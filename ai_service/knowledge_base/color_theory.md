# Colour Theory for Outfit Styling

## Skin tone matching
- Warm undertones (golden, peachy): mustard, rust, olive, warm coral, camel, gold jewellery suit best.
- Cool undertones (pink, bluish): jewel tones (emerald, sapphire blue, magenta), true reds, silver jewellery suit best.
- Neutral undertones: most colours work; soft pastels and muted tones are safest, with one bold accent piece.

## Occasion-based palettes
- Birthday / celebratory daytime: pastel yellow, lilac, blush pink, mint — light, photogenic, not overly formal.
- Birthday evening / dinner: jewel tones, deep wine, emerald, navy with metallic accents — reads dressier under low light.
- Festive / traditional: rich reds, maroons, golds, royal blues.
- Casual outing: denim-friendly tones — white, sage, terracotta, soft stripes.

## Pairing rules
- One statement colour + one neutral (white, beige, black, navy) keeps an outfit from feeling busy.
- Monochrome (same colour family, different shades) reads elegant and works well for photos.
- Avoid combining two competing bold prints; if the outfit has a print, keep shoes/jewellery solid.

## Budget-tier styling notes
- Under ₹1500: focus on fit and one good accessory (belt, statement earrings) rather than chasing trend pieces.
- ₹1500–₹3500: room for a well-cut fit-and-flare or A-line dress in a flattering colour, plus matching footwear.
- ₹3500+: consider a dress with a design detail (puff sleeves, embroidery, structured waist) and a coordinated jewellery set.
