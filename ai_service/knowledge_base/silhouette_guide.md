# Silhouette & Body-Type Guide

This guide is descriptive, not prescriptive — the goal is to give the styling agent
grounded language for *why* a silhouette is suggested, not to box anyone into a category.

## Common silhouette goals
- Define the waist: fit-and-flare, wrap dresses, belted A-line.
- Lengthen the leg line: empire waist, high-low hems, vertical seam details, nude/matching footwear.
- Balance broader shoulders: V-neck or sweetheart necklines, A-line skirts.
- Add structure to a straighter frame: peplum details, gathered waists, structured puff sleeves.

## Dress lengths by occasion
- Mini/above-knee: casual day events, younger crowd birthday parties.
- Midi: most versatile — birthday dinners, brunches, semi-formal.
- Maxi: evening events, festive occasions, photoshoots.

## Sleeve & neckline notes
- Puff sleeves and balloon sleeves are currently strong trend pieces for birthday/party dressing (2025-26 seasons).
- Halter and one-shoulder necklines pair well with statement earrings; skip a necklace with these.
- High necklines pair well with drop earrings and a delicate bracelet rather than a bold necklace.

## Footwear pairing logic
- Block heels: safer for outdoor/standing-heavy events, still dressy.
- Strappy heels/sandals: best with midi/maxi lengths, evening events.
- Flats/sneakers: casual or daytime only; avoid with structured party dresses unless intentionally styled "dressed down."
