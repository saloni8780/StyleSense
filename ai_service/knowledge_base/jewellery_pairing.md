# Jewellery & Accessory Pairing Rules

## General rule
Pick ONE focal point: either statement earrings, a statement necklace, or a statement bracelet/clutch.
Everything else stays minimal. Two loud pieces compete for attention in photos.

## Metal matching
- Gold jewellery: warmer outfit tones (mustard, rust, maroon, olive, cream).
- Silver/white jewellery: cooler tones (navy, emerald, lilac, icy pastels) and most black outfits.
- Rose gold: flexible — works with blush, dusty rose, lilac, and most pastels.

## Coordination checks for uploaded pieces
When reviewing a photo set of outfit + shoes + jewellery together, check in this order:
1. Formality match — does the jewellery's formality level match the outfit (e.g. delicate chain vs structured party dress)?
2. Metal-tone match — gold vs silver vs rose gold against the outfit's dominant colour.
3. Colour clash — does any piece introduce a competing bold colour not present elsewhere in the outfit?
4. Footwear formality — do the shoes match the dress length and event formality (see silhouette guide)?
5. One clear fix — if something is off, the fix should be the smallest change that resolves it (e.g. swap one
   accessory) rather than a full re-do.
